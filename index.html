LUCENT · QUICK GUIDE

Make the lamp yours.

Customize the lamp

Open Lamp Studio to change the vessel, wax and liquid colors, wax amount, blob scale, flow speed, and glow. Choose Small, Medium, or Large beneath the lamp.

Change a Mood

Use the color picker in the top bar to change the background. Choose a curated mood or generate a new combination with the randomizer.

Play your own music

Select the folder icon to open an MP3 from your device. Press play to start the track. Use the orange spectrum to see the sound across the player, adjust volume with its slider, and pause or resume with the play button. Hover over the player for an orange particle burst; the effect stays the same size in fullscreen. Drag the track name to move the player, or drag its toggle when the player is collapsed.

Physics Simulation

Open Lamp Studio and switch to Physics Simulation to try the separate Paper.js lava animation. The size buttons and lamp studio controls also affect the simulation. Use its base power and play/pause buttons to control the animation; switch back to return to the classic lamp.

Fullscreen

Select the expand icon to enter fullscreen. Select it again, or press Escape, to leave fullscreen.

Refresh and water waves

Use the refresh icon to reload the page. When focus is not in a control, press Space to pause or resume the water-wave animation; if it has finished, Space plays it again. On a small screen, use the water icon to replay the wave and the eye icon to show or hide the Mac-style dock. The GSAP Developer scrubber also controls its playhead on desktop.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press H to show or hide the GSAP Developer Tools scrubber. Press M to show or hide the macOS-style dock. Shortcuts are ignored while using a button, slider, or text field.
